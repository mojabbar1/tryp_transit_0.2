### How to use
- Paste each slide’s "AI Slide Prompt" into your slide generation tool.
- Keep bullet points concise on slides; use Speaker Notes for narration.
- Replace placeholders like <CITY>, <PILOT_PARTNER>, <METRIC> before exporting.
