### Market size
- **Q**: How big is this market?
- **A**: TAM = urban transit riders × trips per year × attachable spend per trip. We target metros with dense station-area merchants. Replace assumptions per metro and recompute.

### Traction and PMF
- **Q**: What traction do you have?
- **A**: MVP live; tracking nudge CTR, trip→redeem rate, and partner ROI. Pilots with <CITY>/<AGENCY> planned; LOIs pending.

### Unit economics
- **Q**: What’s CAC and LTV?
- **A**: CAC via station-area and event activations; LTV from recurring trips and merchant fees. Redemption costs controlled via windows and thresholds.

### Defensibility
- **Q**: Why can’t others copy?
- **A**: Contextual timing and verified-transit reward loop, plus local partner network density near hubs.

### Privacy & security
- **Q**: How do you handle data securely?
- **A**: Server sessions (Auth.js), RBAC, validated inputs, rate limits, secrets hygiene, and PII-scrubbed logs. Minimal PII retention.

### Regulatory/age gating
- **Q**: Alcohol rewards and age restrictions?
- **A**: Age verification and region-specific controls. Non-alcohol alternatives are equivalent.

### GTM
- **Q**: How do you scale merchants and agencies?
- **A**: Start with nightlife/food near stations; weekly events; agency pilots with shared KPIs.

### Technical scalability
- **Q**: Can this scale across cities?
- **A**: Yes—multi-tenant architecture planned; partner offers and nudges are city-configurable.

### Competitive landscape
- **Q**: Who are the main competitors?
- **A**: Generic transit apps and generic rewards; our transit-verified, context-timed approach is distinct.

### Milestones
- **Q**: What are the next 12-month milestones?
- **A**: Pilot KPI achievement, 100+ merchant partners across 2 metros, agency dashboard v1, and positive unit tests.
