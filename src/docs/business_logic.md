# Business Logic for Rewards System

## Reward Thresholds & Rationale

| Reward Type      | Required Trips | Estimated Value | Business Rationale                                    |
|------------------|----------------|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| eCredit          | 3              | $3              | Low barrier entry, immediate utility, habit formation |
| Free Coffee      | 5              | $3-5            | Daily habit reinforcement, morning commute focus     |
| Free Appetizer   | 6              | $6-8            | Social dining encouragement, weekly engagement       |
| **Free Beer**    | **7**          | **$5-7**        | **Premium positioning, evening/weekend targeting, maximum memorability** |

## Beer Reward Strategic Positioning

### Why 7 Trips?
1. **Premium Experience**: Higher threshold creates perceived value
2. **Evening/Weekend Pattern**: Less frequent than daily coffee consumption
3. **Marketing Impact**: "Work all week, earn your beer" narrative
4. **Investor Memorability**: Higher threshold justifies "flagship" positioning

### Time-Based Beer Contexts
- **Friday 3PM+**: "TGIF_HAPPY_HOUR" - Weekend celebration framing
- **Weekend Anytime**: "WEEKEND_RELAXATION" - Leisure and social framing  
- **Weekday 4-10PM**: "WEEKDAY_UNWIND" - Post-work relaxation framing
- **Late Night 10PM-1AM**: "LATE_NIGHT_SOCIAL" - Social activity framing
- **Other Times**: "GENERAL_BEER_CONTEXT" - Generic beer messaging

## Success Metrics
- **User Engagement**: Trip completion rates by reward type
- **Partner Value**: Foot traffic and redemption rates
- **Viral Potential**: Social sharing of beer rewards
- **Investor Appeal**: "Free beer app" brand recognition
